Living lightly, connected deeply, with a private home and a whole village of shared spaces.

This newly built 2024 ground-floor condo is designed for everyday comfort: an open living area, abundant natural light, accessible kitchen and bathrooms, and peaceful bedrooms set within a car-free garden courtyard community.

The home is part of Treehouse Village Ecohousing, a 16-acre intergenerational community with shared dining, workshop, coworking, gym, guest rooms, gardens, forest, EV charging, and direct access to the Centennial Trail.

Condo fees are $477/month and include heat, electricity, water, internet, snow removal, and garbage. Sewage is approximately $500/year separately.
