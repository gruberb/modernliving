01 · The home

The home

A quiet, light-filled apartment on the ground floor, with room to breathe and a whole village just outside the door.

Built in 2024, this three-bedroom, two-bath condo is designed for easy daily life: an open living area, big windows, an accessible kitchen and bathrooms, and bedrooms set away from the noise. It sits in a car-free garden courtyard, so the first thing you meet outside is people and trees, not parking.

The home is part of Treehouse Village Ecohousing, a 16-acre intergenerational community with a shared dining hall, workshop, coworking lounge, gym, studio, guest rooms, gardens, forest, EV charging, and direct access to the Centennial Trail.

The $477 monthly condo fee covers heat, electricity, water, internet, snow removal, and garbage. Sewage runs roughly $500 a year on top of that.

02 · The village

The village

Cook for one, or host for thirty.

Beyond your own kitchen, the community kitchen and great hall give you space for big dinners, birthdays, and shared meals without cramming everyone into the apartment. Bring the celebration; leave the cleanup to a room built for it.

A workshop, a studio, a place to work.

Work from the coworking lounge, build something in the workshop, get a session in at the gym, or make art in the studio. The everyday extras most condos never offer are a short walk across the courtyard.

Warm in winter. Cool in summer. Quiet all year.

Passive House construction wraps the home in a tight, well-insulated envelope, so it stays comfortable on far less energy. Lower bills, steadier temperatures, and a noticeably calmer indoor feel.
