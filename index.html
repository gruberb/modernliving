New to cohousing?

You own your home. The village adds the rest.

Think private home plus shared resources. You own your fully equipped condo, with your own kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living space, and front door. You also get access to the spaces most homes only need occasionally: the Common House and community kitchen, guest rooms, a coworking lounge, gym, studio, workshop, gardens, forest trails, EV charging, and carshare. Connection is there when you want it; privacy is yours when you need it.

01 · The home

The home

A quiet, light-filled ground-floor condo, with room to breathe and a whole village just outside the door.

Built in 2024, this 3-bedroom, 2-bath condo is designed for easy daily life: an open kitchen, dining, and living area; generous windows; an accessible kitchen and bathrooms; and bedrooms tucked away from the main living space. Step outside into a car-free garden courtyard, where neighbours, gardens, and trees are the first things you meet, not parking.

The home is part of Treehouse Village Ecohousing, an intergenerational community set on 11 acres of forest and community gardens, with a shared dining hall, workshop, coworking lounge, gym, studio, guest rooms, EV charging, and direct access to the Centennial Trail.

The $477 monthly condo fee covers heat, electricity, water, internet, snow removal, and garbage. Sewage runs roughly $500 a year on top of that.

02 · The village

The village

Your kitchen for everyday meals. A common kitchen for the big ones.

Beyond your own kitchen, the community kitchen and great hall give you space for big dinners, birthdays, and shared meals without cramming everyone into your condo. Bring the celebration; leave the cleanup to a room built for it.

Work, make, move, host, without needing extra rooms at home.

Work from the coworking lounge, build something in the workshop, get a session in at the gym, or make art in the studio. The everyday extras most condos never offer are a short walk across the courtyard.

Warm in winter. Cool in summer. Quiet all year.

Built to Passive House standards, the home sits inside a highly insulated, airtight envelope, so it stays comfortable on far less energy. Lower bills, steadier temperatures, fewer drafts, and a noticeably calmer indoor feel.

Good to know

Questions buyers ask

Cohousing is a private home with shared extras. Here is what that means in practice.

Is this shared housing?

No. This is a privately owned condo in a cohousing community. You have your own kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living space, and front door.

Do I have to join shared meals or events?

No. Shared meals, events, and projects happen when residents organize them. Community is available; it is never required.

What does the condo fee cover?

The $477/month fee covers heat, electricity, water, internet, snow removal, and garbage. Sewage is billed separately at roughly $500/year.

Can guests stay over?

Yes. The community has reservable guest rooms, so visitors do not always need to stay inside your condo.

Can I buy now and move later?

Possibly. We are open to discussing a rental arrangement for a buyer planning a future move. Ask us and we will talk it through.

Curious about the community?

An established, well-documented village

Treehouse Village has been covered by CBC, Radio-Canada, Maclean's, Canadian Press, and Passive House Accelerator. Read more about the community, its shared spaces, and its sustainability at treehousevillage.ca.
