A private, accessible Passive House condo with a whole village of shared spaces outside the door.

This move-in-ready ground-floor home is part of Treehouse Village Ecohousing, Atlantic Canada's first cohousing community. The open living area, natural light, accessible kitchen and bathrooms, and quiet bedroom placement make the apartment comfortable for daily life.

Condo fees include heat, electricity, water, internet, snow removal, and garbage. Shared amenities include the great hall, community kitchen, workshop, coworking lounge, gym, studio, guest rooms, gardens, forest trails, EV charging, and EV car sharing.
