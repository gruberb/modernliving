A private, accessible Passive House condo with a whole village of shared spaces outside the door.

This move-in-ready ground-floor home is part of Treehouse Village Ecohousing, Atlantic Canada's first cohousing community. The open living area, natural light, accessible kitchen and bathrooms, and quiet bedroom placement make it comfortable for daily life.

The monthly condo fee covers heat, electricity, water, internet, snow removal, and garbage. Shared amenities include the great hall, community kitchen, workshop, coworking lounge, gym, studio, guest rooms, gardens, forest trails, EV charging, and EV car sharing.
